How this is calculated
Academic year minimums (34 CFR 668.3)
At least 30 weeks of instructional time for credit-hour programs (26 with an ED-approved reduction) and 26 weeks for clock-hour programs. Undergraduate programs also need at least 24 semester or trimester hours, 36 quarter hours, or 900 clock hours.
Week of instructional time
Seven consecutive days with at least one scheduled day of instruction or exams. Weeks run Monday through Sunday. A mid-week start, such as the Tuesday after Labor Day, counts as week 1. Every term closes out its last week, and the next term starts at the beginning of a later week, never mid-week. When weekends count, a term ends on Sunday. When they don't, it ends on Friday. A week that is entirely a break has no instruction, so it isn't counted and the term runs a week longer. A partial-week break doesn't remove the week.
Standard terms
Semesters are typically 14–17 weeks, and two make an academic year. Trimesters are typically 14–17 weeks, and three make an academic year: the summer trimester is required and its weeks count toward the year. Quarters are typically 10–12 weeks, and three make an academic year. Each term is a payment period.
Summer quarter header or trailer
A quarter program can add an optional summer quarter. As a trailer it is the last payment period of the academic year it follows. As a header it is the first payment period of the academic year it starts. Either way it is an extra payment period: its weeks don't count toward the 30-week minimum, and the same summer can't serve as both a trailer and a header.
Nonstandard terms
Any term that isn't a semester, trimester, or quarter. Terms are substantially equal when no term differs from another by more than 2 weeks. SE9W terms are substantially equal and at least 9 weeks each. Each term is a payment period, and a student must finish both the weeks and the credits of the academic year before starting the next one.
Next academic year start
With "Same weekday, one year later", the next year starts on the same weekday as the first start, in the week nearest the anniversary. A Monday, August 24, 2026 start gives Monday, August 23, 2027, never a Sunday or Tuesday.
Repeating breaks
Breaks you enter are copied into each later year on the same weekdays in the week nearest the original dates, so Thanksgiving week stays Monday through Sunday. Terms that contain a break are extended so each still has its full weeks of instruction. If you enter a break's real date for a later year, that entry replaces the copy.
Non-term and clock-hour programs (34 CFR 668.4)
The academic year ends at the close of the later of two weeks: the week the student completes the weeks of instruction, and the week they complete the hours at the scheduled pace. Hours accrue per instructional day, so a week shortened by a break earns fewer. Payment period 1 ends with the later of half the weeks and half the hours. Payment period 2 and the next academic year start the following Monday, so years never overlap.
Projected dates assume the student attends as scheduled. For non-term and clock-hour programs, actual completion of hours can move the end date. Confirm results against your school's approved academic year definition.
